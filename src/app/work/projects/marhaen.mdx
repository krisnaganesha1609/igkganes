# Marhaen Digital Cashier — Multi-Role POS & Backoffice App

**Marhaen Digital Cashier** is a mobile-based point-of-sale and backoffice application designed to support daily operational workflows in small-to-medium food and beverage businesses. While conceptually similar to NEVMOCK (RASA KOPI Backoffice), this application introduces **authentication, role hierarchy, and more granular inventory control**.

The system focuses on reliability, role separation, and accurate stock tracking through ingredient-based calculations.

---

## Project scope
- Application type: Mobile POS & Backoffice  
- Domain: Food & Beverage Operations  
- Target users: Café and restaurant staff  
- Focus: Role-based workflows and inventory accuracy  

---

## Supported roles

### Junior Staff
- Create and process customer orders  
- View menu availability  
- Limited access to operational data  

### Senior Staff
- All Junior Staff permissions  
- Manage orders and order status  
- Update menu items and pricing  
- Monitor ingredient stock levels  

### Owner
- Full system access  
- View business analytics and summaries  
- Manage users and role assignments  
- Oversee inventory and supply management  

---

## Core features
- **Multi-role authentication**
  - Login system with role-based access control
- **POS & Order Management**
  - Create, update, and track customer orders
- **Menu Management**
  - CRUD operations for menu items
- **Ingredient-Based Inventory**
  - Define ingredients per menu item
  - Automatic stock deduction based on orders
  - Supply stock monitoring and alerts
- **Analytics Dashboard**
  - Sales summary and operational insights
- **User Management**
  - Role assignment and access control

---

## Tech stack
- **Mobile:** Flutter  
- **State Management & Navigation:** GetX  
- **Architecture:** Feature-based modular structure  
- **Local & Remote Data:** REST API integration  

---

## Architecture overview
1. **Presentation Layer**
   - Flutter UI
   - GetX controllers for state and navigation
2. **Application Logic**
   - Role-based feature gating
   - Order and inventory calculation logic
3. **Data Layer**
   - API services for authentication, orders, and inventory
   - Models representing menu, ingredients, and supply stock

GetX is used to provide fast iteration, simplified state handling, and centralized navigation.

---

## Technical challenges
- Designing ingredient-based stock deduction logic  
- Preventing negative stock during concurrent orders  
- Managing UI complexity across multiple user roles  
- Balancing simplicity with feature richness in POS workflows  

---

## Impact & lessons learned
- Role separation improves operational clarity and reduces user errors  
- Ingredient-level inventory significantly increases stock accuracy  
- GetX enables rapid development but requires discipline to maintain scalability
